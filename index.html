Who we are

We bring the lab to the classroom

Project Uddipon (উদ্দীপন — “inspiration” or “ignition”) is a nationwide STEM empowerment initiative by ZAN Tech. Our engineers design and deliver free, high-quality workshops on Robotics, Programming, AI/ML and IoT for students across Bangladesh.

“No talented Bangladeshi student should be left behind in the Fourth Industrial Revolution — regardless of geography, gender or income.”

  • check_circle 100% free for students and institutions
  • check_circle Delivered by practising ZAN Tech engineers
  • check_circle Nearly half of all participants are girls
  • check_circle STEM club setup and mentorship afterwards

What we do

Six programmes, one goal: students who build

Every session is hands-on and project-based, shaped to the students' age and level. No long lectures — just building, coding and discovering.

How it works

From your first message to a room full of robots

Five simple steps — almost all of it handled by our team.

01

You reach out

Message us with your institution, location, student count and preferred dates.

02

We plan together

Within 48 hours we confirm topics, format, date and on-site logistics.

03

We arrive equipped

Engineers bring every kit, laptop and material — at zero cost to you.

04

Students build

A 3–6 hour hands-on session where every student builds, codes and tests.

05

Club & support

We help start a STEM club and keep mentoring online afterwards.
